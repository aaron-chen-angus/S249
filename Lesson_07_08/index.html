LESSON 7–8 · MEASURES OF DISPERSION

How Spread Out?

Range

Largest − smallest. Quick, but uses only 2 values.

Standard deviation

How closely data cluster around the mean. Smaller SD = more precise.

Standard error (SEM)

SEM =SD√n

How much the sample mean would vary between samples.

    LESSON 8 · PROBABILITY · P-VALUE · SIGNIFICANCE

    Chance or Real?

    01 ROLL THE DICE

    Probability of rolling any one number = 1/6 ≈ 16.7%. Does a few rolls match? Do many?

    Independent events

    One does not affect the other. e.g. having a lung-cancer gene and choosing to smoke.

    Dependent events

    One changes the chance of the other. e.g. study time and final grade.

    02 P-VALUE vs SIGNIFICANCE LEVEL (α)
    H₀No significant difference in salary of polytechnic graduates by gender.
    H₁There is a significant difference by gender.

    p ≤ α → reject H₀ (significant). p > α → fail to reject H₀.
