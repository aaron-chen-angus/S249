LESSON 9 · WHAT IS A T-TEST?

Signal ÷ Noise

A t-test asks: is the gap between two means big compared with the spread of the data?

–t CALCULATED
–t CRITICAL
–df
–p-VALUE

Rule 1 · t

|t calculated| > t critical → reject H₀

Rule 2 · p

p < 0.05 → reject H₀

LESSON 10 · TYPES OF T-TEST

Three Tests

01 ONE TAIL OR TWO?

Two-tailed

H₁ says “different” or “change” – either direction.

One-tailed

H₁ says “increase”, “decrease”, “more”, “less”, “reduce”.

02 INDEPENDENT · EQUAL OR UNEQUAL VARIANCES?

Similar group sizes (e.g. 118 vs 126) → Two-Sample Assuming Equal Variances · T.TEST type 2

Very different sizes or spreads (e.g. 12 vs 35) → Two-Sample Assuming Unequal Variances · T.TEST type 3
